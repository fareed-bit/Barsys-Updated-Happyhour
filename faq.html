What’s included?

Classic, Signature and Reserve include two hours of bar service, machines, bartending, mixers, cocktail and mocktail options, setup and cleanup. Menu and garnish inclusions vary by package. Glassware and venue logistics are confirmed separately.

Who provides the alcohol?

Spirits and mixers are included in your Happy Hours package. The event team confirms your drink selections and final details in your proposal.

How many guests can you host?

Published instant estimates cover 20–250 guests. For smaller or larger events, share your headcount with the team for a custom quote.

Are zero-proof drinks included?

Yes. Cocktail and mocktail options are included in full-service packages. A dedicated zero-proof station can be requested separately.

Where do you host?

The service is centered on New York City. Tell us about your office or venue; the team confirms availability, access and delivery for your location.

Does an inquiry reserve my date?

No. An inquiry starts the conversation. The team confirms availability, the final scope, agreement and required payments before an event is booked.

Can this become a regular event?

Yes, you can request a recurring office happy hour. Share your preferred frequency with the team; availability and terms are confirmed in your proposal.
